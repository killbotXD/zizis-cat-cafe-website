Our Story

01 — THE PLACE

A living room that happens to serve coffee

Up a flight of stairs in Indiranagar, Zizi's is built to feel like a lived-in home: warm lamps, walnut panelling, a battered sofa by the big glass window and a cabinet full of vintage board games. Pull up a chair, stay a while, and let a cat decide whether you're worthy of their lap.

02 — THE FOOD

Good food with a bit of personality

No mainstream café filler. Think khow suey, omurice and bibimbap, tortilla-crust pizzas, shakshuka, akuri eggs, granola bowls and a whole matcha bar. There's plenty for vegans, vegetarians and everyone else, made fresh with lots of healthy choices.

03 — THE CATS

Every cat here is waiting for a home

Our resident cats are rescues, and some of them are up for adoption. Spend time with them, get to know their quirks, and if one picks you, we'll walk you through adopting. The cats have their own quiet spaces and never go into the kitchen, and we ask guests not to feed them.

FAQ

Do I need to book a table?
Walk-ins are welcome. On weekends, tables are held for 90 minutes so everyone gets a turn with the cats.
Can I adopt one of the cats?
Some of our resident cats are up for adoption. Ask the team and we'll introduce you, tell you about each cat's personality, and walk you through the process.
Can I feed or pick up the cats?
Please don't feed them. They're on a careful diet. Let the cats come to you, and don't pick up anyone who isn't in the mood.
Do you have vegan and vegetarian options?
Lots. The menu is marked vegan, vegetarian and egg/chicken, and many dishes come in more than one version.
Are the cats allowed in the kitchen?
Never. The kitchen and service areas are cat-free. The cats live in the seating area and have their own quiet room.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday, 12 pm to 8 pm. Saturday and Sunday, 10:30 am to 8 pm.
Are you hiring?
We're looking for people who love food and cats: baristas, cooks and floor staff. Drop us a message below.
